# Human review guide

Review the evidence in this order:

1. `evidence/repaired_standalone/`
2. `evidence/repaired_production_profile/`
3. `evidence/dawnhewn_compatibility/`
4. `evidence/dawnhewn_production_profile/`
5. `evidence/cabin_slider/`
6. `evidence/cabin_repair_activation/`
7. `evidence/manual_sparse_cabin/`

For every comparison, distinguish visible change from successful repair. Ask:

- Is the intended semantic effect visible?
- Does it scale with weight?
- Does the repair suit the material and remain functional?
- Is the same subject preserved, or has it become a redesign/replacement?
- Did unrelated geometry, background, style, age, or wear change?
- Is the apparent effect larger than matched control variance?

The production-profile sheets refine the initial result; they do not invalidate it. The original and sparse prompts also differ, so prompt sparsity and generation settings are not individually isolated.

No machine evaluator verdict is represented as human judgment in this package. The complete interpretive record is in `CUSTOMER_REPORT.md`.
